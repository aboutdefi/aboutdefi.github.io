Binance buys $100 million Circle stake in five-year USDC deal

Binance bought $100 million of Circle shares and signed a five-year USDC promotion deal, with monthly fees tied to balances held through Circle’s wallet service.

Hashline EditorialSep 24, 20262 min read

Binance invested $100 million in Circle and agreed to promote USDC on its platform under a five-year commercial deal announced September 22, 2026, according to Circle’s announcement. The agreement expands USDC access, particularly in emerging markets, and pairs the exchange’s promotion with infrastructure services from Circle.

The investment and commercial agreement both closed on September 17, according to Circle’s SEC filing. The filing says Binance bought 1,237,011 shares of Circle’s Class A common stock for $80.84 each, bringing Circle $100 million through a private placement.

How will Binance promote USDC under the deal?

Binance agreed to undertake activities promoting USDC on its platform, while Circle will pay a monthly incentive fee based on the amount of USDC held through Circle’s Modular Smart Contract Wallet service, the SEC filing says. Circle’s announcement says the companies will focus on expanding access in emerging markets and that Circle will provide infrastructure for holding and using USDC.

The filing does not disclose the fee rate or the monthly payment amounts. It says either company may end the five-year arrangement early if specified events occur.

What did Binance pay for its Circle shares?

The $80.84-per-share price reflected a discount to Circle’s market price before the sale, the filing says. Circle’s announcement puts the discount at 5% and says Binance agreed not to transfer the shares for up to two years after closing, subject to customary exceptions.

The filing says the restriction also covers pledging or hedging the shares, with specified exceptions. Binance retains its rights as a Circle shareholder, including the right to vote its shares during the restricted period.

Which earlier USDC agreements does this replace?

The new arrangement replaces agreements Circle entered into with Binance in November 2024 and August 2025, according to the filing. The five-year term is subject to the early termination provisions in the new agreement.

Circle described the agreement as focused on USDC promotion and access, while Binance co-CEO Richard Teng said the company’s investment and five-year commitment represented long-term conviction, according to Circle’s announcement. The filing sets out the commercial mechanism: Binance promotes USDC, and Circle’s monthly incentive fee is tied to USDC held through its wallet infrastructure.
