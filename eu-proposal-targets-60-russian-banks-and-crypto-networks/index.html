EU proposal targets 60 Russian banks and crypto networks

The European Commission proposed sanctions on Nov. 23 targeting 60 Russian banks and crypto networks, tightening financial pressure and barring Russian teams from EU sports.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 25, 2026 · 13:58 UTC2 min read bd192e

The European Commission proposed a 22nd package of Russia sanctions on Nov. 23, targeting 60 Russian banks, crypto and payment networks, and Russian teams and athletes competing under the country’s flag at EU sports events, according to its statement on the proposed package.

How would the proposal affect crypto?

The Commission said the measures would crack down on Russian crypto and payment networks and include third countries that help Russia evade sanctions. President Ursula von der Leyen said the EU had launched a Crypto Sanctions Task Force to support that effort.

The proposal targets crypto platforms as part of measures to restrict how Russia finances its war in Ukraine. The Associated Press report said countries helping Russia evade sanctions could be caught by the crypto measures.

What financial measures are included?

The Commission proposed extending transaction bans to about 60 Russian banks and removing all 60 from SWIFT, the international payments messaging system. The proposal also expands restrictions to energy services and lists more than 70 vessels, according to von der Leyen’s statement.

What does it propose for sport?

The package would bar Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag at sports events in the EU. It also proposes banning official Russian symbols at those events, restricting Russian sponsors’ financial flows to European sports and cultural associations, and limiting Russians’ roles in European sports federation governing bodies.

When could the measures take effect?

The package is a proposal and must be endorsed by all 27 EU member countries before it can take effect. The AP reported the approval process can take weeks and that the measures may change during negotiations.
