How does Shibarium change SHIB fees and wallet use?

What is Shibarium used for? It handles lower-cost SHIB transfers and swaps, with BONE for gas; see how it compares with Ethereum and when to switch.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 27, 2026 · 08:17 UTC3 min read b746d7

Shibarium is the Shiba Inu ecosystem’s Ethereum layer-2 network for lower-cost transfers and swaps, with BONE paid as gas. It launched in 2023; the project’s launch recap dates the mainnet launch to August 16.

Ethereum and Shibarium use separate networks, even though both work with Ethereum-compatible wallets. Shibarium processes transactions on its own chain, and validators periodically submit checkpoints to Ethereum. The official network details list BONE as Shibarium’s currency symbol.

That changes the fee and the token you need. Sending SHIB on Shibarium costs a BONE gas fee, generally lower than an ETH fee for a comparable action on Ethereum; the exact fee varies. Your wallet address stays the same when you switch networks, but its balances are network-specific.

That distinction matters after bridging. Say you bridge SHIB from Ethereum into Shibarium: the SHIB arrives in the wallet’s Shibarium balance, but the wallet needs BONE to pay for a transfer or swap. Without BONE, the bridged SHIB sits idle—you can see it, but you cannot move it on Shibarium until you add gas.

ShibaSwap, a decentralized exchange, runs on Ethereum and Shibarium. Using shiba swap on Shibarium means a swap uses BONE for gas, which can suit frequent small trades; on Ethereum, the gas token is ETH. Choose the network where your tokens are held, and keep that network’s gas token available.

Use Shibarium for everyday SHIB transfers and smaller swaps when lower fees matter and you have BONE. Use Ethereum when your tokens or intended transaction are there. Moving assets between the networks requires a bridge, and that step also has network fees.

  • Shibarium handles transactions separately and checkpoints to Ethereum.
  • Shibarium gas is paid in BONE; Ethereum gas is paid in ETH.
  • Keep BONE in the Shibarium wallet before bridging SHIB if you plan to move or swap it.

What readers ask next

How do I bridge SHIB to Shibarium?

Use the Shibarium bridge to transfer SHIB from Ethereum, and have ETH ready for the Ethereum-side fee. After it arrives, you need BONE to transact on Shibarium.

How do I add Shibarium to MetaMask?

Add Shibarium as a network in MetaMask using the network details published in the official Shibarium documentation. Check that MetaMask shows Shibarium before sending or swapping tokens.

Where can I get BONE for Shibarium gas?

Obtain BONE on a supported network and move it to your Shibarium wallet. Confirm the network and token before sending; BONE held only on Ethereum will not pay Shibarium gas.

Is Shibarium safe to use?

Shibarium uses its own validator and checkpoint system linked to Ethereum. Review the bridge and wallet details carefully before transferring assets, since using a bridge adds a cross-network step.
