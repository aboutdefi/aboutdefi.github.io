CFTC Clears Backpack and tZERO Derivatives Venues

The CFTC designated exchange operators for Backpack and tZERO and registered their clearing affiliates, opening a regulated route for new derivatives markets.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 24, 2026 · 11:54 UTC2 min read 2f5e59

The U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission designated Backpack’s exchange operator on Nov. 16 and registered its clearing affiliate on Nov. 13, while tZERO received its exchange designation Nov. 10 after an October clearing approval. The CFTC’s Backpack exchange filing lists TLD Markets, Inc. as designated.

Backpack operates through TLD Markets and TLD Clearing, which does business as Backpack Clearing. The CFTC’s clearing record says TLD Clearing may clear margined futures, fully collateralized futures, options on futures and swaps. The exchange designation and clearing registration give the two entities separate regulatory roles in a derivatives market.

tZERO’s clearing affiliate, tZERO DCO, LLC, was registered on Oct. 16 to clear fully collateralized futures, options on futures and swaps. The CFTC designated tZERO DCM, LLC as a contract market on Nov. 10. The Block reported that CFTC Chair Michael Selig described the approvals as a pathway for market infrastructure built using blockchain technology.

The registrations establish regulated exchange and clearing entities, but do not by themselves confirm that trading has begun. Backpack said its planned U.S. venue is intended to offer leveraged crypto perpetual futures, 24-hour trading, crypto collateral and cross-margining through one global order book. Those are company-stated plans, not details listed in the CFTC’s filing.

tZERO said its approvals allow it to develop exchange and clearing services for financial institutions and other market participants, including derivatives products subject to further regulatory approvals. The next step for both firms is to develop their offerings within the scope of their registrations and any applicable product approvals.
