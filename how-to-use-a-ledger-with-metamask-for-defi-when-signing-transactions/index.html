How to use a Ledger with MetaMask for DeFi when signing transactions

Learn how to use a Ledger with MetaMask for DeFi: connect your Ethereum account, review each request on the device and confirm transactions there.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 28, 2026 · 14:45 UTC3 min read 58c705

You can use a Ledger with MetaMask for DeFi by connecting its Ethereum account in MetaMask and approving each transaction on the Ledger device. Your seed phrase stays on the Ledger; MetaMask receives public addresses and builds transactions for you to review.

A USDT bridge moves USDT from one blockchain to another, so using one is a DeFi action where you should review the transaction details before signing. The same device approval process applies to swaps, lending and other dApp transactions.

What should you have ready?

Have your set-up Ledger, Ledger Live, MetaMask and a Chromium browser ready. Update the Ledger firmware and install the Ethereum app through Ledger Live before connecting the device.

Close Ledger Live when the Ethereum app is installed. MetaMask connects to the Ledger through the browser, and leaving Ledger Live open can interfere with that connection.

How do you connect a Ledger and sign a DeFi transaction?

Connect the hardware wallet in MetaMask, select its account, then approve a test transaction on the device. The selected Ledger account appears separately from your existing MetaMask accounts, so funds must be sent to that Ledger address before you can use them from it.

  1. Install the Ethereum app. In Ledger Live, update firmware if needed and install the app. The Ethereum app can sign for EVM networks you add in MetaMask. Result: the app is available on your Ledger.
  2. Close Ledger Live and open the Ethereum app on the device. Result: the Ledger is ready for MetaMask to connect.
  3. Choose Add hardware wallet, then Ledger, in MetaMask. Allow the browser to access the device when prompted. Result: MetaMask can read the Ledger’s public addresses.
  4. Select the account you want to use. MetaMask adds it as a separate account. Send funds to that address to use them for DeFi. Result: the Ledger account is available in MetaMask.
  5. Start with a test transaction and review the Ledger screen. Approve it on the device only after checking the details. Result: once confirmed on the Ledger, the transaction appears on the blockchain explorer.

Pay close attention to signing settings. Blind signing shows only a hash on the Ledger, so you cannot review the transaction’s details on the device. Some dApp actions may require it; enable it in the Ethereum app settings only when needed, and use a dApp with clear signing when available. Clear signing lets the Ledger display details such as the spender and amount before you approve.
