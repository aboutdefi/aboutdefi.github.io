How to pay with xDai on Gnosis Chain from your wallet

How to pay with xDai on Gnosis Chain: select the network, send xDAI to the recipient’s address, keep extra for gas, and track confirmation on GnosisScan.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 27, 2026 · 10:51 UTC2 min read f38f47

Select Gnosis Chain in your wallet before sending xDAI, because the network determines where the payment is processed. Enter the recipient’s address and amount, then sign the transaction; xDAI is Gnosis Chain’s native coin and pays the network fee.

You need a wallet with enough xDAI for both the payment and gas, plus the recipient’s address. If your xDAI is on Ethereum, move it to Gnosis Chain first; Gnosis Bridge can move tokens between the networks. For context on the payment side, read Gnosis Bridge’s role in Gnosis Chain payments.

How do you send xDAI from your wallet?

A payment is a wallet transaction from your Gnosis Chain address to the recipient’s address. Select Gnosis Chain in MetaMask, then confirm the wallet shows chain ID 100 before you send.

Paste the recipient’s address and check it carefully against the address they gave you. Enter the xDAI amount, leaving extra xDAI in your wallet for gas. The recipient gets the amount you entered; the network fee is charged separately to you.

Review the network, recipient, amount and displayed fee before signing. Once you sign, MetaMask submits the transaction to Gnosis Chain. Use the transaction link in your wallet or search the transaction on GnosisScan to see its status.

How much xDAI do you need, and what happens next?

You need more xDAI than the payment amount because gas comes out of your balance separately. The fee can vary with transaction conditions, so use the fee shown in your wallet and keep enough extra to cover it.

Say you owe a friend 5 xDAI. Enter 5 as the payment amount and leave additional xDAI in your wallet for the fee; the friend receives 5 xDAI if the transaction completes. After signing, share the transaction link if they want to follow the confirmation on GnosisScan.

The same steps work when paying a merchant or sending funds to another Gnosis Chain address: check the network, verify the address, reserve xDAI for gas, and review before signing. If you need funds from Ethereum, use Gnosis Bridge to move them to Gnosis Chain, then return to the wallet to make the payment.

  • Open MetaMask and select Gnosis Chain (chain ID 100).
  • Check the recipient address and enter the xDAI amount.
  • Leave extra xDAI for gas, review the fee, and sign.
  • Follow the transaction on GnosisScan.
