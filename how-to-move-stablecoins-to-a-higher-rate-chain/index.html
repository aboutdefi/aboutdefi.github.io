How to Move Stablecoins to a Higher-Rate Chain

Compare stablecoin supply rates across chains, calculate whether the gap covers bridge costs, then move your funds and supply them on the destination market.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 28, 2026 · 07:11 UTC3 min read bf2be3

Compare supply rates for the same stablecoin across chains first; the rate gap determines whether moving your funds can repay its costs. To move USDC or USDT, withdraw it from your current market, bridge it to the target chain, then supply it there. The receipt token, such as an aToken, appearing in your wallet confirms the move worked.

Jumper bridge finds routes through supported bridges and swaps to transfer assets between networks. For a Base-to-Arbitrum move, the Jumper bridge route from Base to Arbitrum changes which source and destination chains you use; compare the rates and costs for that route before withdrawing.

What should you have ready?

Have an EVM wallet, gas on the source chain, and a small amount of gas on the destination chain. Know which token the destination market accepts: native USDC and bridged USDC.e are separate assets with separate markets and rates.

How do you move stablecoins to a higher-rate market?

  1. Compare supply rates for the same token. Check the lending app or DefiLlama for the token’s supply APY on each chain you are considering. Aave V3, for instance, runs on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism and Polygon. Its supply rates change with pool utilization, so use current rates and compare USDC with USDC, or USDT with USDT. You should have a rate gap between your current and target markets.
  2. Calculate the break-even time. Estimate the round-trip bridge cost, then divide it by the extra monthly interest: break-even months = round-trip bridge cost ÷ (amount × rate gap ÷ 12). A 2% rate gap on 1,000 USDC earns about 1.67 USDC a month. If the round-trip cost is high relative to that, a small balance may take a long time to repay the move.
  3. Withdraw from your current market. Redeem the supplied stablecoin in the lending app and wait for it to appear in your wallet. The available token balance is ready to bridge.
  4. Bridge to the target token and chain. Select the source asset, destination chain and token in Jumper bridge, review the route and estimated costs, then confirm. Circle CCTP burns USDC on the source chain and mints native USDC on the destination; other routes may deliver a different supported token, such as USDC.e. The destination balance should match the asset accepted by your chosen market.
  5. Supply on the destination lending app. Choose the matching market, supply the bridged funds and confirm the transaction. The task is done when the receipt token, such as an aToken, shows in your wallet on the destination chain; it confirms your stablecoins are supplied there.
