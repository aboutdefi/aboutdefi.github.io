About Meridian

Meridian covers crypto markets, DeFi infrastructure, token networks, regulation, and security incidents. Its reporting focuses on the decisions, code changes, and incentives that shape digital finance beyond daily price noise.

The publication is edited by Elena Voss, a Chicago-based journalist who spent eight years covering blockchain infrastructure and fintech for specialist technology outlets. She reads primary documents, speaks with builders and researchers, and keeps Meridian focused on clear, accountable reporting.

Meridian is independent by design. Coverage is guided by evidence, useful context, and respect for readers who want to understand how crypto systems work.
