About Blocktape

Blocktape reports news from across crypto: exchanges and markets, protocol upgrades, token launches, security incidents, and the regulators and courts shaping the industry. Every story carries its publication time and a short ID, and opens with the fact that matters.

Stories rely on primary sources — filings, on-chain records, official statements and direct confirmation — and say plainly when something is unconfirmed. Errors are corrected in the story with a dated note. Blocktape is independent and not paid by the projects it covers; nothing published here is financial advice.
