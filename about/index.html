About Hashline

Hashline reports the news across crypto: markets and exchanges, protocol upgrades, regulation and enforcement, security incidents, funding and the people behind the projects. We cover many projects and are tied to none of them.

Every story starts from a primary source — filings, official statements, on-chain records or named spokespeople — and says where each fact comes from. When we get something wrong, we correct it on the article and note the change. Hashline is independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
