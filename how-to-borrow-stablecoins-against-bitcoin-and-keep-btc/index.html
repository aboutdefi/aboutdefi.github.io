How to Borrow Stablecoins Against Bitcoin and Keep BTC

How to borrow stablecoins against Bitcoin in DeFi: wrap BTC as WBTC on Ethereum, supply it as collateral, and borrow DAI or USDS while tracking liquidation risk.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 24, 2026 · 08:02 UTC3 min read 0b64da

To borrow stablecoins against Bitcoin in DeFi, convert BTC to WBTC on Ethereum, supply it as collateral, then borrow DAI or USDS below the market’s LTV limit. On SparkLend, that route adds a lending-market step: you supply listed WBTC and borrow a listed asset against it. Starting with BTC in an exchange or wallet, you finish with stablecoins in your wallet and a health factor you can monitor.

Move from BTC to a stablecoin balance

Borrowing against BTC means wrapping it before it can serve as collateral in an Ethereum lending market.

  1. Set up an Ethereum wallet and keep ETH in it for gas. Native Bitcoin cannot be used directly by Ethereum contracts, so you need WBTC, an ERC-20 token backed 1:1 by custodied BTC.

  2. Obtain WBTC on Ethereum. You can withdraw it from an exchange that supports Ethereum withdrawals or use a wrapping route that takes BTC and issues WBTC. Check the network before sending: WBTC on another chain is not the Ethereum asset you need for an Ethereum market.

  3. Account for the cost and time of getting in and out. Wrapping and later unwrapping can involve mint and redeem fees, plus processing delays at both ends. Include those costs and delays in your borrowing plan; they affect how much value you keep and how quickly you can return to native BTC.

  4. Connect your wallet to SparkLend and supply WBTC as collateral. Review the market’s listed assets and borrowing parameters before confirming. Supplying collateral does not sell your BTC exposure, but the WBTC position is now securing the loan.

  5. Choose DAI or USDS and borrow well below the maximum shown. LTV, or loan-to-value, sets the borrowing limit against your collateral; it is a ceiling, not a target. Leaving room below it gives the position more capacity to absorb a fall in collateral value or other market movement.

  6. Confirm the transaction, then check the dashboard for both your stablecoin balance and health factor (HF). HF below 1 means the position is eligible for liquidation, so keep it above 1 and monitor it as WBTC value and loan conditions change.

Questions about borrowing against Bitcoin

Bitcoin-backed DeFi borrowing depends on the wrapper, the lending market, and the position’s health factor.

Can I borrow against native Bitcoin without wrapping?

Not on an Ethereum lending market that requires Ethereum collateral. Native BTC cannot be supplied to Ethereum contracts, so this route uses WBTC; other systems may support BTC through different mechanisms.

Is borrowing against crypto a taxable event?

Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction and circumstances. Borrowing may be treated differently from selling, but wrapping, redeeming, liquidation, or other transactions can have separate tax consequences; check local rules with a qualified tax professional.

WBTC vs tBTC: which should I use?

They are different Bitcoin-backed Ethereum tokens with different mechanisms. Check which asset the lending market lists, how it is backed, and how its minting and redemption work before choosing collateral.
