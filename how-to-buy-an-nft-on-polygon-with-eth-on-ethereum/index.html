How to buy an NFT on Polygon with ETH on Ethereum

How to buy an NFT on Polygon with ETH held on Ethereum: bridge ETH to Polygon PoS, get POL for gas, approve WETH and confirm the NFT in your wallet.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 21, 2026 · 06:42 UTC3 min read e1688a

To buy a Polygon NFT with ETH held on Ethereum, bridge ETH to Polygon PoS, get a little POL for gas, then buy through a marketplace such as OpenSea.

What to have ready

Have a wallet such as MetaMask with Polygon PoS added, ETH for Ethereum gas, and enough ETH to cover the NFT price. Check the listing currency before moving funds: a WETH listing uses bridged ETH, while a POL listing means you need POL for the purchase.

For a WETH listing, ETH deposited through the Polygon bridge arrives on Polygon PoS as WETH, an ERC-20 token. If the listing is priced in POL, bridge the ETH and swap enough WETH to POL on Polygon before buying; keep a little POL aside for gas.

Buy the NFT step by step

  1. Check the listing currency. Open the NFT listing and note whether its price is in WETH or POL. This tells you whether to keep the bridged WETH or swap some for POL.
  2. Deposit ETH to Polygon. Connect your wallet to the Polygon bridge, choose Ethereum as the source and Polygon PoS as the destination, then deposit the amount you need. The deposit uses ETH for gas on Ethereum; the funds are credited on Polygon in minutes.
  3. Get POL for gas. Make sure your Polygon wallet has a small POL balance before trading. You need POL to pay Polygon PoS transaction fees, including the WETH approval and the NFT purchase if the listing uses WETH.
  4. Connect to the marketplace. Open the listing on OpenSea or another marketplace that supports Polygon, connect your wallet, and confirm that the marketplace is using Polygon PoS. The listing and payment must be on Polygon for this purchase.
  5. Approve WETH, then buy. For a WETH listing, approve the marketplace to use WETH from your wallet; this is a separate transaction from buying. Confirm the approval in your wallet, then submit and confirm the purchase. Budget POL for both transactions. For a POL listing, make sure you have enough POL for the price and gas, then confirm the purchase.
  6. Confirm the NFT arrived. After the purchase completes, switch your wallet to Polygon PoS and check its collectibles. The task is done when the NFT appears there.

The Polygon bridge covers moving supported assets between Ethereum and Polygon PoS, which is the step that puts the payment on the network where the NFT is listed. Once the NFT is in your Polygon wallet, the next question is whether to leave it there or move it across networks.

What happens in other cases?

How is the NFT moved to Ethereum later?

To move the NFT to Ethereum, start a withdrawal through the Polygon bridge and select Ethereum as the destination. Follow the bridge flow until the NFT is available in your Ethereum wallet.

What fees are paid in total for this path?

This path includes ETH gas for the Ethereum deposit and POL gas on Polygon for the WETH approval and purchase. The total varies with network conditions and the listing; check the wallet prompts and marketplace checkout for the amounts before confirming.
