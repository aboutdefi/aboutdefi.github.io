How to Convert Bitcoin to WBTC for BTC Holders

How to convert bitcoin to WBTC: trade BTC for WBTC on an exchange, then withdraw it to your Ethereum wallet; direct minting is for approved merchants.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 18, 2026 · 15:48 UTC2 min read 85c702

Bitcoin is usually converted to WBTC by trading BTC on an exchange, then withdrawing WBTC to an Ethereum wallet.

  • Deposit BTC to an exchange that lists WBTC.
  • Trade BTC for WBTC and withdraw it on Ethereum.
  • Only approved merchants can mint WBTC directly.

WBTC is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum with 8 decimals, backed 1:1 by bitcoin held in custody. In the merchant route, an approved merchant delivers BTC to the custodian, BitGo, and receives minted WBTC.

RenBridge used a different bridge model, letting users initiate cross-chain transfers directly; it is no longer active. To learn more about the chains and hosts involved, see our article on which blockchains RenBridge supported and which hosts.

Individuals get WBTC through an exchange

For most BTC holders, the exchange route is the practical way to get WBTC into an Ethereum wallet. You need BTC in a wallet, an exchange account that lists WBTC, and an Ethereum wallet with ETH to pay network gas.

Before trading, open WBTC’s transparency page. It publishes custodian bitcoin addresses and the WBTC supply, so you can compare the BTC held at those addresses with the amount of WBTC in circulation. This gives you a public reserve check before you convert.

  1. Start with BTC in your wallet. Open an account at an exchange that supports both BTC deposits and WBTC trading. Confirm that the exchange accepts deposits on the Bitcoin network.
  2. Deposit BTC to the exchange. Copy the exchange’s Bitcoin deposit address and send BTC from your wallet. Match the network and address carefully; wait for the exchange to credit the deposit before placing a trade.
  3. Trade BTC for WBTC. Use the BTC/WBTC market if available, or the exchange’s supported route between the two assets. Review the quoted amount and trading fee before submitting the order.
  4. Withdraw WBTC on Ethereum. Choose Ethereum as the withdrawal network and enter your own Ethereum wallet address. Do not select another network: WBTC on a different chain may not arrive as the Ethereum ERC-20 token you need. Allow time for the exchange and Ethereum network to process the withdrawal.
  5. Add WBTC and confirm receipt. Add the official WBTC contract for Ethereum to your wallet so it can display the token. Confirm that the balance appears in the wallet and that the withdrawal transaction shows the WBTC transfer on Etherscan.
