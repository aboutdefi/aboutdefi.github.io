How to Pay an Onchain Invoice With Monero

An onchain invoice paid with Monero usually requires converting XMR to its named asset, then sending it on the specified network before expiry.

The Blocktape Editors Oct 3, 20262 min read b1ed5c

To pay an onchain invoice with Monero, send XMR directly if the invoice accepts it; otherwise, convert XMR to the required asset and pay on the named network. An invoice’s asset, network, amount and expiry determine the route.

Can you pay an onchain invoice directly with Monero?

You can pay directly only when the invoice specifies Monero and provides a valid XMR address or payment request. In Monero GUI, you can paste a request beginning with monero:, check the amount and address, then confirm the transaction.

If the invoice asks for another asset, such as a token on a different chain, an XMR transaction alone will not settle it. You need a conversion route that delivers the requested asset to the invoice’s destination address; see this guide to choosing an XMR bridge for your wallet for how to assess that route.

What should you check before sending XMR?

Read the invoice for its exact asset, network, destination address, amount and deadline. A token name by itself is not enough: the same token can exist on different networks, and a transfer sent to the wrong network may not reach the invoice’s recipient.

  • Confirm whether the invoice accepts XMR or requires a conversion.
  • Match the requested token and network exactly, and copy the invoice’s destination address.
  • Check the quoted destination amount, conversion fees, minimums and expiry time before authorizing a route.
  • Keep enough XMR to cover the sending wallet’s transaction fee as well as the amount being converted.

Monero’s official payment guide says the sender pays the XMR transaction fee, which varies with network congestion and transaction size rather than the amount sent. If a conversion is involved, the route may also charge fees or deliver a different amount when the quote expires or market prices change.

How do you complete and confirm the payment?

For a direct XMR invoice, paste its address or payment request into a synchronized Monero wallet, verify the amount and recipient, and confirm the send. The Monero wallet reports whether the transaction is pending or included in a block; its guide says confirmation takes about two minutes on average, but can take longer.

For an invoice requiring another asset, follow the route’s instructions to send XMR and provide the invoice’s destination details. Check the received asset on the specified network and the invoice’s own status; an XMR transaction ID proves the Monero transfer, but does not by itself show that the separate invoice payment arrived.

Monero transactions cannot be reversed after confirmation, so verify the address and amount before sending. Once the route reports delivery, check that the invoice has registered payment before treating it as settled.
