US wins authority to seize $1.2 billion in Binance hack funds

The Justice Department won court authority to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion of Binance hack proceeds; tracing and forfeiture work continues.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 16, 2026 · 15:37 UTC2 min read d594a2

The Justice Department obtained court authority to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance, the department said Thursday, Nov. 12. The FBI has attributed the theft to North Korean military hackers it calls TraderTraitor, according to the department.

The $1.2 billion is part of a larger pool of assets that investigators and crypto companies have tracked since the Oct. 29 theft. The Justice Department’s figures show the scale of the recovery effort so far:

  • More than $6 billion in virtual assets was stolen from Binance, the department said. The Associated Press reported the FBI put the theft at about $6.3 billion.
  • More than $2.7 billion of the stolen funds has been frozen through combined US government and private-sector efforts, according to the Justice Department.
  • More than $1.2 billion of that frozen amount is covered by court authorization to seize or restrain it. The order does not mean all of those assets have been forfeited or returned to victims.

The Justice Department said the effort involves its National Security Division and Criminal Division, the US Attorney’s Office for the District of Columbia, and law enforcement partners working with virtual asset service providers. The FBI previously urged those providers to block transactions involving addresses used by TraderTraitor to launder the Binance funds.

Investigators are still tracing and freezing assets, and the department said the hackers continue moving funds through cryptocurrency bridges, mixers and exchanges. The Associated Press reported that the US government typically files a forfeiture claim with a judge, who then determines who has a legitimate claim to recovered funds. The Justice Department said its work to trace, seize and forfeit the stolen assets is ongoing.
