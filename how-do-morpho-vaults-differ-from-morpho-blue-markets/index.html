How do Morpho vaults differ from Morpho Blue markets?

Morpho vaults pool one asset across curated Morpho Blue markets; direct lending picks one market, giving lenders more control and a concentrated risk choice.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 27, 2026 · 23:44 UTC3 min read 809fb0

After weighing lending routes, Morpho vaults spread a lender’s deposit across curated Morpho Blue markets, while direct supply puts it into one market. The choice is between delegated allocation and control over a specific lending pair.

A direct supplier chooses the loan asset, collateral, oracle and LLTV of a single market. For a broader comparison of Morpho vault deposits and how they work, Morpho vaults are a useful next move.

Morpho vaults spread deposits across markets

Morpho vaults put a pool of one asset under a Curator’s strategy, then an Allocator moves funds among eligible markets. The Curator sets the risk framework; the Allocator handles deployment within it.

Each Morpho Blue market is an isolated pair with one loan asset, one collateral asset, an oracle, an interest rate model and an LLTV, all fixed at creation. A vault sits above these markets and can allocate its deposits across several of them.

Say a USDC vault allocates to wstETH/USDC and WBTC/USDC markets. Its depositors get exposure to both; a direct USDC supplier picks one market and takes that market’s risk alone.

That spread changes the lender’s exposure, not the underlying market rules. Vault depositors give up choosing collateral market by market, but still take a pro-rata share of bad debt from every market the vault supplies. In Morpho Blue, bad debt is realised immediately and shared among that market’s suppliers, so a vault’s share of a loss depends on its allocation there.

Markets fit lenders who want to select and monitor collateral and oracle themselves. Vaults fit lenders who prefer a Curator and Allocator to manage diversification and allocation, while accepting their choices and the risks of each underlying market. Borrowers use markets; vault depositors are lenders.

Before choosing, compare the markets in a vault’s strategy, their collateral and oracles, and the vault’s Morpho vault APY alongside its exposure and liquidity. That gives you a basis to choose between hands-on market selection and delegated allocation.

Four Morpho lending questions

These answers cover curator choice, market creation, audits and oracle failure.

Who are the biggest Morpho vault curators?

There is no permanent ranking: vault size can change over time. Compare the assets managed by current vaults, then assess each Curator’s strategy and risk decisions.

How do I create a Morpho Blue market?

Market creation is permissionless. A creator fixes the loan asset, collateral asset, oracle, interest rate model and LLTV at creation.

Are Morpho vaults audited?

Audit status depends on the specific vault version and contracts involved. Check the deployment and its underlying adapters and markets; an audit does not remove market or oracle risk.

What happens if a Morpho market’s oracle fails?

Price-dependent actions such as borrowing and liquidation may stop while the oracle is unavailable. Supplying or withdrawing may still work, but a lender’s exit depends on available liquidity.
