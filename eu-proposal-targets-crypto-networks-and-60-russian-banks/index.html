EU proposal targets crypto networks and 60 Russian banks

The European Commission’s 22nd Russia sanctions package would target crypto and payment networks, extend bans to about 60 banks and bar Russian-flag teams from EU sports.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 26, 2026 · 13:15 UTC2 min read d18dcd

The European Commission on Nov. 23 proposed a 22nd sanctions package against Russia, targeting crypto and payment networks and about 60 banks, according to Ursula von der Leyen’s statement on the proposal. The measures would also bar Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag at sports events in the EU.

Crypto and payment networks are a focus

Von der Leyen said the package would crack down on crypto and payment networks in Russia and in third countries that help Russia evade EU sanctions. She also said the Commission had launched a Crypto Sanctions Task Force to support that work.

The statement did not name specific crypto platforms or describe the proposed restrictions in detail. The Commission said it wants to align its work on crypto and payment networks with international partners.

About 60 banks would face transaction bans

The proposal would extend transaction bans to about 60 Russian banks and remove all 60 from SWIFT, the international financial messaging system, according to the Commission. The Associated Press report also said the package targets crypto platforms and sporting participation.

The Commission also proposed listing more than 70 ships in Russia’s “shadow fleet” and banning tankers insured in Russia, according to von der Leyen’s statement. Other measures include a proposed visa ban for Russian ex-combatants and restrictions on Europeans holding posts in Russian companies.

EU member states must approve the package

The Commission’s announcement is a proposal, not an implemented sanctions package. The Associated Press reported that all 27 EU member countries must endorse the measures before they can take effect, a process that could take weeks and may change the proposals.
