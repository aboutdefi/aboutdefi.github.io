US wins authority to seize $1.2B in Binance theft

The Justice Department says courts authorized seizure or restraint of more than $1.2 billion in Binance theft proceeds, within $2.7 billion frozen so far.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 16, 2026 · 18:25 UTC2 min read 7ccbd2

The U.S. Justice Department said on Nov. 12 that it had obtained court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance on or about Oct. 29. The department’s announcement said the FBI attributes the theft to North Korean military members known as TraderTraitor.

The $1.2 billion is part of a larger pool of funds frozen through U.S. government and private-sector efforts. The Associated Press report described the FBI action as a seizure; the Justice Department specified that courts authorized seizure or restraint of more than $1.2 billion.

  • More than $6 billion: The Justice Department says that amount was stolen from Binance. The AP reported the theft at about $6.3 billion.
  • More than $2.7 billion: The amount frozen so far through collective U.S. government and private-sector efforts, according to the Justice Department.
  • More than $1.2 billion: The amount for which the department says it has court authorization to seize or restrain assets.

After the theft, Justice Department teams worked with virtual-asset service providers to interdict the stolen funds. The agency said TraderTraitor actors continue laundering assets through cryptocurrency bridges, mixers and exchanges, while efforts to trace, freeze, seize and forfeit the funds continue.

The FBI, U.S. Secret Service and IRS Criminal Investigation issued an alert last week urging virtual-asset providers to block transactions involving addresses used to launder the Binance funds, the Justice Department said. The AP reported that a forfeiture claim typically goes before a judge, who determines who has a legitimate claim to recovered funds.
