When Are Ethereum Gas Fees Lowest for Sui? Timing a Deposit

Ethereum gas fees are lowest when block demand is low; there is no reliable hour. Compare a live estimate with the Suibridge quote before signing.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 26, 2026 · 07:01 UTC2 min read 8b1900

Ethereum gas fees for a Suibridge transfer are lowest when fewer users compete for block space, but there is no dependable hour or day to start. Fees change with demand, so compare a live estimate with the deposit quote and proceed when the total cost fits your budget.

A small transfer can make the deposit fee a larger part of the amount moved. That makes the gas cost of a small transfer a useful case for Suibridge: its lock-and-mint deposit uses Ethereum gas, while Sui gas applies to transactions on Sui.

Ethereum fees fall when block demand eases

Ethereum gas prices move with activity on the network. Under EIP-1559, the base fee adjusts with block demand and can rise by up to 12.5% in a full block. A quiet moment may bring a lower estimate, but demand can change at any hour.

That makes a tracker useful for spotting a potentially cheaper moment, not for reserving a price. Its estimate is a snapshot; the base fee may rise before the transaction is included. Weekends or overnight hours are not a reliable promise of lower fees.

Compare the live estimate with the deposit quote

Use the tracker to see current Ethereum gas conditions, then use the wallet quote to assess the deposit you plan to make. The quote gives the expected cost for that transaction; check it again just before signing because the fee can change before inclusion.

  1. Prepare an Ethereum wallet with the asset you want to bridge and ETH on Ethereum mainnet for gas.
  2. Look up the current Ethereum gas estimate. Treat it as a snapshot of network conditions.
  3. Open Suibridge and review the deposit quote for your intended amount.
  4. Recheck the quote before signing. Submit the deposit only when the expected cost fits your budget.

Say the tracker estimate is lower during a quieter period. Open the deposit quote and compare the expected cost with what you are willing to pay; if the quote has risen or no longer fits, wait and compare again later. A low tracker reading alone is not a reason to sign.

Ethereum gas fees are lowest when block demand is lower, and that can change at any time. Check the live estimate, verify the deposit quote immediately before signing, and let the quoted cost decide when to start.
