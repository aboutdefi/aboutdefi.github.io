US wins authority over $1.2B in stolen Binance crypto

The Justice Department says a court authorized seizure or restraint of more than $1.2 billion in Binance funds, part of a wider $2.7 billion freeze.

The Blocktape Editors Nov 20, 2026 · 14:55 UTC2 min read fa74f3

The U.S. government has court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance, the Justice Department said Nov. 12. The department’s announcement said the FBI attributed the theft to TraderTraitor, a group it identifies as North Korean military members.

The authorization covers part of a larger amount frozen after the theft. The Associated Press reported that the theft totaled about $6.3 billion and that the U.S. government and private-sector partners had frozen more than $2.7 billion.

  • More than $6 billion stolen: The Justice Department said the theft occurred on or about Oct. 29. The amount marks the scale of the reported breach.
  • More than $2.7 billion frozen: That is the total the department says U.S. agencies and private-sector partners have frozen so far.
  • More than $1.2 billion under court authority: The department says it has obtained authorization to seize or restrain this portion of the frozen assets. That wording does not mean all of it has been forfeited.

The distinction matters for anyone tracking whether stolen funds have been returned. The AP reported that the U.S. government typically files a forfeiture claim with a judge in such cases, who then determines who has a legitimate claim to recovered funds.

The Justice Department said efforts to trace, freeze, seize and forfeit the Binance assets are ongoing. It said TraderTraitor members continue to launder funds through cryptocurrency bridges, mixers and exchanges, and that the FBI has urged virtual asset providers to block transactions linked to the addresses being used.
